1. Introduction and design basis
1.1 Design objective
The design provides a variable air volume supply-air layout for the given open-plan office.
It covers the preliminary cooling load, maximum and minimum diffuser flows, classroom
air-diffusion checks, VAV connections and rectangular duct sizing required by the
assignment.
1.2 Adopted office and system assumptions
The supplied plan annotation of 1022 m2 is used for the rule-of-thumb load calculation.
The interpreted CAD boundary is used for diffuser locations and directional lengths. These
are different quantities: the annotated area is not presented as a surveyed net area. The
adopted layout has 80 diffusers, twenty VAV groups and 47 rigid supply sections.

1.3 Calculation scope
The empirical allowance and routing allowance are preliminary design assumptions. This
report distinguishes the classroom ratio check from a measured ADPI percentage. Full fan
selection and detailed system pressure are supplementary engineering studies, not part of
the assignment duct-sizing calculation.
