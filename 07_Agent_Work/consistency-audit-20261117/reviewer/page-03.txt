3. Diffuser selection and air-diffusion checks
3.1 Selected diffuser and characteristic length
The selected Krueger 1400/51400 has a 600 x 600 mm panel, 8-inch neck and no damper
[2]. Both operating flows of all 80 diffusers lie within its published table. T0.25 is taken
from the third horizontal isothermal throw value; intermediate flows use linear
interpolation between adjacent published rows.
Characteristic length L is taken in each principal plan direction to a boundary or the
midplane between opposing outlets, as an explicit assumption of the planar design. The 320
directional lengths comprise 40 boundary-limited and 280 opposing-outlet cases. Appendix
B records all four lengths for every diffuser.
3.2 Maximum and minimum operating cases
All 640 directional operating cases lie within the adopted classroom interval T0.25/L =
1.0-3.4. Original catalogue interpolation gives 1.1526-3.0598; the independent native
K-Select check gives 1.2117-3.1122 [3]. Figure 1 shows the directional extremes for each

3.3 Interpretation and design approximation
The preliminary sensible allowance is 170.1 W/m2, 6.3% above the printed classroom table
limit of 160 W/m2. Using the classroom interval at this allowance is an explicit coursework
design approximation. It is not a teacher-issued exemption, a prediction of ADPI >=80%,
or a transfer of the manufacturer Type 3 curve [4]. No measured ADPI percentage is
claimed.
