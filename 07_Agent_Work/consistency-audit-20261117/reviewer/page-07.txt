5. Design assessment and conclusion
5.1 Consistency of the adopted design
The report, diffuser schedule, VAV grouping and duct schedule refer to one 80-diffuser
design. The CAD was saved and reopened in AutoCAD; generated geometry and all 80
straight connection lengths were checked against the calculation sources. The reviewed A1
plot uses a 1:100 scale, with a 100 mm reference measured at 99.991 mm. The formal
drawing is included after the appendices.
5.2 Independent calculation checks
Native K-Select GRD reports cover all 160 terminal operating flows through 49 distinct
whole-CFM inputs. Inputs differ from the prescribed flow by no more than 0.2331 L/s.
Independent PDF-coordinate checks reconcile the reported conditions and results. The
source imperial throw units are converted to metres; a low-flow NC dash remains qualified
unknown. These checks support numerical consistency, not installed performance
certification.
5.3 Conclusion and limitations
The adopted design provides 12000.373 L/s maximum and 4800.149 L/s minimum supply
air through twenty four-outlet VAV groups. All classroom directional ratio checks meet the
selected interval under the stated load-range approximation. The 47 rigid duct sections
meet the adopted sizing criteria, and the conditional flexible-connection budget is below
the stricter 4 m design cap.
The result is a preliminary coursework design. Its principal limitations are the empirical
cooling allowance, table-range approximation, interpreted plan boundaries and routing
allowance. Actual ceiling conditions, final installed lengths and product installation would
require confirmation before construction. Full-system pressure and fan selection remain
separate engineering work; supplementary linear-slot and CFD studies do not replace the
specified 1400/51400 coursework design.
