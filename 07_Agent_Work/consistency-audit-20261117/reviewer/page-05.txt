SD-56 receives 155.9466 L/s at maximum flow and 62.3786 L/s at minimum flow. Its
northward L increases from 1.3940 to 1.4690 m. At the unchanged native input of 330
CFM (155.7427 L/s), the reported 15.0 ft throw gives T0.25 = 15.0 x 0.3048 = 4.5720 m.
Hence T0.25/L = 4.5720/1.469042 = 3.1122, compared with 3.2797 before the adjustment
[3].
The original metric-table interpolation is retained separately: T0.25 = 4.4903 m at the
prescribed maximum flow, giving 4.4903/1.469042 = 3.0566 [2]. The native and catalogue
values use different flow rounding and reported performance; they are not presented as
identical.
The minimum-flow limiting point after adjustment is SD-08 eastward: native T0.25 =
2.7432 m and L = 2.263959 m give a ratio of 1.2117. All other directions and both
operating flows are checked in the schedules; improving SD-56 alone would not establish a
whole-layout pass.
