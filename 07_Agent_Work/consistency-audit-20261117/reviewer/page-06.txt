4. Duct sizing and Hong Kong design provisions
4.1 Rigid duct arrangement and sizing
The single-line plan connects the VAV groups to a branched supply trunk using straight
sections where practical. Each of the 47 sections is identified on the drawing and in
Appendix D. Rectangular area determines actual air velocity; the adopted main/branch
speed limits are 10/8 m/s. The upper friction criterion is 1.23 Pa/m and the preferred
aspect-ratio limit is 4:1.
All 47 adopted sections were checked in the assignment-linked Online Ductulator using 36
unique input combinations [5]. D06, D13 and D20 use 550 x 350 mm. The schedule retains
both the online friction value and an independent equivalent-round calculation; the two are
not assumed numerically identical. Section friction is a pressure gradient, not total fitting,
terminal or fan pressure.
