2.2 Diffuser distribution and VAV grouping
Each diffuser receives its assigned share of the total airflow. The 80 maximum flows sum
to 12000.373 L/s, and every minimum flow is exactly 40% of its maximum. Each of the
twenty VAV groups serves four diffusers, satisfying the assignment requirement for four to
five outlets per box. Group flows equal the sum of their diffuser flows; the complete
grouping is given in Appendix C.
Twelve diffusers are displaced 150 mm south within their original explicit service areas.
Service-area loads, all terminal flows, VAV membership and rigid section sizes remain
unchanged; Figure 2 identifies the adjusted row.
2.3 Flexible connection design
The longest straight XY connection reconstructed from the saved/reopened drawing is
2.873 m. Adding the stated 0.50 m routing allowance gives 3.373 m. This leaves a
minimum conditional margin of 0.627 m to the adopted 4 m design cap, which is stricter
than the assignment limit of 5 m. The allowance is not a measured vertical offset or
installed slack.
Flexible ducts are specified as final connections, not main distribution. Final routing must
remain within the ordinary limit and must not pass through fire-resisting barriers or attach
directly to fire/fire-smoke dampers [1]. The actual installed lengths and fire-boundary
classification would be confirmed before installation.
