Appendix E. Supplementary load-basis comparison
E.1 Purpose and status
The main coursework design, drawings and schedules retain the 189 W/m2 classroom
allowance, 0.90 sensible factor and specified 1400/51400 family. The assignment requests
a rule-of-thumb load; the classroom guide offers 189 W/m2 as a preliminary reference. This
appendix examines another explicit assumption. It does not replace the main design or
remove its ADPI applicability limitation.
A Hong Kong office study reports a whole-floor cooling load of 129 W/m2, excluding
safety/standby allowance [7]. Applying a declared 15% design margin gives 148.35 W/m2,
rounded upward to 150 W/m2. This is a coursework scenario, not a measured load for this
building. The study concerns one office floor with 1999 measurements; its 2001
publication does not establish a universal or current design allowance.
