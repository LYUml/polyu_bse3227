INTRODUCTION
The Krueger 1240/51240 series modular core diffusers 
are one of Krueger’s most quiet and versatile diffusers, 
combining excellent performance with on-the-job 
flexibility. The 1240/51240 series diffusers exhibit typical 
isothermal throws of 20’ to 30’ for 4-way air distribution 
and 40’ to 60’ for 1-way air distribution. The modular cores 
in the neck maintain a tight horizontal air pattern at the 
ceiling from maximum to minimum CFM, making this series 
an excellent selection for variable air volume applications. 
The modular cores are factory set for a four-way air pattern, 
but may be field adjusted for 1-, 2- or 3-way air patterns. 
The modular capability makes the 1240/51240 ideal for 
installations with changing air pattern requirements such 
as tenant lease spaces.
