\reportheading{5. Design assessment and conclusion}
\subhead{5.1 Consistency of the adopted design}
The grid location schedule, drawing and appendices identify the same 80 outlets and 20 groups. The appended single-line drawing includes the revised 47-section sizing schedule.

\subhead{5.2 Independent calculation checks}
For the adopted design, the 160 operating flows are interpolated within the published 33–297 L/s metric table. The manufacturer lists isothermal throw and sound under catalogue conditions. Installed noise, VAV controllability and total system pressure require separate checks.

\subhead{5.3 Conclusion and limitations}
The adopted design provides 13091.316 L/s maximum and 5236.526 L/s minimum supply air through twenty four-outlet VAV groups. Minimum-flow ratios meet the classroom criterion. At peak flow, SD-77 eastward and SD-78 westward slightly exceed its upper bound. The 47 rigid duct sections meet the adopted sizing criteria, and the conditional flexible-connection budget is below the stricter 4 m design cap.

The result is a preliminary coursework design. Its principal limitations are the empirical cooling allowance, table-range approximation, interpreted plan boundaries and routing allowance. Actual ceiling conditions, final installed lengths and product installation would require confirmation before construction. Full-system pressure and fan selection remain separate engineering work; supplementary linear-slot and CFD studies do not replace the specified 1400/51400 coursework design.
