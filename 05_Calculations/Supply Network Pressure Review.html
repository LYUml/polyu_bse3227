Separate engineering study; full pressure and fan duty remain unknown. This is not a merged design with the relocated VAV Connection Layout scenario. Original course sources and designs are preserved.

Thirteen enlarged branch-plus-taper arrangements are replaced by reduced-branch sharp tees while retaining external end planes, original outlet demands and the source requirement inventory. The saved rigid model contains 89 bodies,197 ports,88 connections and 20 outlet paths. All 88 native material cap pairs match. The same unwaived numerical policy finds zero solid intersections and 88 clearance violations at connected interfaces; this does not establish installation clearance.

Both maximum and given 40 percent minimum flow conserve at every port and product. There are 63 fitting paths:29 with conditional source values and 34 without. The original model had 76 paths,31 conditional values and 45 gaps. Removing 13 tapers removes 3 previously populated values and 10 unknowns; one revised branch obtains a new bounded source value. Neither component reduction nor missing-data reduction proves a lower complete pressure.

At minimum flow, elbow Reynolds numbers and source corrections are recalculated. One previously plateau-only binding now uses the same publisher printed 12 correction row for lower Reynolds numbers. Independent Decimal arithmetic agrees; maximum-flow values are unchanged. No bounds are extrapolated.
